About

Stats student. Builder. Question asker.

I'm a first-year Statistics & Data Science student at UT Austin. I like taking a messy spreadsheet, asking it a sharp question, and coming back with a chart that actually answers it.

I work mostly in Python and R, and lately I've been building with AI agents, like a crew of them I run from my phone. Right now I'm learning statistical modeling: regression, resampling, and spotting when a model is lying to me.

How I work

  1. 01

    Ask a sharp question

    Pin down what decision the data should help with before touching it.

  2. 02

    Wrangle & model

    Clean, explore and fit the simplest model that holds up to checking.

  3. 03

    Ship a clear story

    One chart, one takeaway, and the code so anyone can rerun it.
